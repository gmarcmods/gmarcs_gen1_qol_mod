# Gmarc's Gen 1 QoL Mod

A [gen1recomp](https://github.com/bryanthaboi/gen1recomp) mod.
Twelve quality of life mods for Red, Blue and Yellow, packed into one install.

## Features

- **Unique Menu Icons** — party and menu icons for all 151 Pokémon, in full colour by default.
- **Touch Tools** — on-screen speed, save, load, colour, zoom and voxel buttons.
- **Run Mode** — hold or toggle B to run.
- **Quality of Life** — the Gen II EXP bar, the Gen II Pack, field-move prompts, a caught icon on the battle HUD and place names when you enter a town or route.
- **Widescreen Battle Intro** — fullscreen battle flashes and poison pulses, with optional flashless intros and black battle outros.
- **Gen 3 Box** — Ruby/Sapphire-style PC boxes.
- **Useful Bag** — bigger pockets and a sortable, fullscreen bag.
- **Reusable Machines** — TMs are reusable and HM moves can be forgotten.
- **Trade Evolutions** — Kadabra, Machoke, Graveler and Haunter evolve by level.
- **Moveset Plus** — compare all five moves when learning a new one.
- **Move Relearn** — relearn any move a Pokémon could have learned.
- **Widescreen Menus** — party and summary use the whole screen on wide windows.
- **HM Anywhere** — use owned HMs and field TMs without teaching them.
- **All Encounters** — each route carries the wild Pokémon of Red, Blue and Yellow together; choose RED, BLUE, YELLOW or ALL.

Everything is optional; each feature has its own toggle in OPTIONS.

## Trade evolutions

| Pokémon | Evolves into | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kadabra | Alakazam | Level 37 |
| Machoke | Machamp | Level 37 |
| Graveler | Golem | Level 37 |
| Haunter | Gengar | Level 37 |

The level is configurable (`TRADE EVO LEVEL`, 30 to 50).

## Install

Import the ZIP in the mod list, enable it for Red, Blue or Yellow, and restart.
Disable the separate mods it replaces — they can't run together.
