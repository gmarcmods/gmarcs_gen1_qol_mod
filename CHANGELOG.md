# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release. One install for Red, Blue and Yellow:

- Unique Menu Icons (full colour by default), Touch Tools, Run Mode, Quality of
  Life, Gen 3 Box, Useful Bag, Reusable Machines, Trade Evolutions, Moveset
  Plus, Move Relearn, Widescreen Menus and HM Anywhere.
- **Trade Evolutions** is new for Gen 1: Kadabra, Machoke, Graveler and Haunter
  evolve at level 37 (configurable) instead of by trade.
- **Moveset Plus** is new for Gen 1: the forget list compares all five moves
  with type, power, accuracy and PP.
- **Widescreen Menus** is new for Gen 1: party and summary use the extra width
  for a detail pane; a 4:3 window is unchanged.
- Quality of Life 1.4.0 adds the **caught icon** on the battle HUD and **place
  names** when entering a town or route.
